About — the AIAD framework and its author

AIAD (Artificial Intelligence Agent Development) is an open-source meta-framework created by Steeve Evers and published at aiad.ovh. SDD Mode, which this site documents, is its Spec Driven Development operating mode.

What is AIAD?

AIAD is the organisational answer to an operational fact: AI agents have made the old way of building software obsolete. The question is no longer "how to code faster" but "how to think and orchestrate better". AIAD judges neither effort, nor velocity, nor process compliance — it judges the value realised for stakeholders.

The framework rests on seven immutable values — from the Primacy of Human Intent to Human Authorship — four pillars, and an ecosystem of five responsibilities rather than rigid roles.

SDD Mode

SDD Mode is the operational guide for AIAD Product Engineers: a strict cycle Intent → Research → SPEC → Gate → Execution → Validation → Drift Lock, tooled by the aiad-sdd npm package (33 slash commands — 17 /sdd + 16 /aiad —, 9 skills, 4 Tier 1 governance agents complemented by a CRA agent for software products, hooks, CI). See the Big Picture and the getting-started guide.

Positioning in the ecosystem

AIAD integrates the best SDD practices and adds the organisational and human dimension. Tools handle "how to work with agents"; AIAD handles "how to work as a human-AI team":

AIAD is model-agnostic by design: Intent Statements, SPECs and the Execution Gate are independent of the LLM and survive provider changes. Governance lives in the artifacts, not in the vendor. In the era of "agent labs", where each lab pushes its own harness, being model-agnostic protects against lock-in and fills a gap in the governance of intent that harnesses do not address. The 2026 industry validations (Context Engineering formalised by Anthropic, R2Code's −41.7% tokens, Thoughtworks Radar, Tang et al. — 91.49% of 20,574 real sessions requiring explicit human correction —, AWS Strands) are independent confirmations of a prior approach.

For a factual comparison with Spec Kit, Kiro, OpenSpec and BMAD that owns both strengths and weaknesses, see the honest comparison (in French).

Two versioning tracks

AIAD moves at two distinct paces, which should not be confused:

  • the AIAD doctrine — values, responsibilities, artifacts, loops, rituals, governance — is versioned in its own right (currently v1.9) and published on aiad.ovh;
  • the aiad-sdd software — CLI, slash commands, skills, hooks, dashboards — follows the SemVer versioning of its CHANGELOG.

The two numbering schemes are not aligned: "aiad-sdd 1.9" (the arrival of skills) has nothing to do with doctrine v1.9 (agent autonomy, verification, compliance). A doctrine rule may precede its tooling: that is why some features are marked "upcoming" on this site. Both tracks are detailed on the What's New page.

Evidence and limits

AIAD applies its value 5, Uncompromising Empiricism, to itself: the doctrine's external support is gathered in legitimation files that state, for each source, what it actually establishes and what it does not.

  • Execution Gate and Human Authorship — agent action bias measured at 35 to 65%, merge authority "almost exclusively human" across 29,585 pull-request cycles. Figures to be re-checked before any formal citation.
  • Verification — the position "verification is designed, not bought" is a bet, not a demonstrated result: the evidence converges (faster but not better review across 1.02 million pull requests), none of it suffices alone. The figure of 80% of the system prompt removed without loss is a vendor measurement, not audited.
  • Accountability — "an agent is the agent of whoever deploys it" is a thesis (Schneier and Sanders), not the state of the law; the Munich ruling on AI Overviews is provisional and under appeal.
  • 2026 regulatory cases — Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the fine imposed on Uber under Art. 22 (Uber has appealed), the Meta incident (according to press reports); the "CVE-2026-LGTM" story is satirical fiction, cited as such. These files do not constitute legal advice.

Open source and contributing

AIAD applies its value 4, Radical Openness: methodological knowledge is shared, never locked away. The aiad-sdd package is published under the MIT licence (© 2026 Steeve Evers — AIAD Framework). Practices, tooling and field feedback are published at aiad.ovh; the package installs with npx aiad-sdd init (see the CLI reference).

To adopt the framework on an existing project: /aiad init bootstraps the structure from your real code, and /aiad onboard generates the briefing for newcomers.

This site, a demonstrator of the framework

This site applies the rules it documents: static HTML/CSS/JS with no framework, webfont or bitmap image (RGESN sobriety), keyboard navigation, AA contrast and text alternatives (RGAA), local search with no external service (GDPR). Its content is written from the repository's real artifacts — .aiad/, governance, commands, CHANGELOG — never invented.
